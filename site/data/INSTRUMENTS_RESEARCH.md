# Instruments research (21.11.2026)

**Maker identified:** Stonewhistle (Hans Houkes, Lent, Netherlands), ceramic flutes. https://stonewhistle.com/ . Heard as "Stone Whistle"; only exact match found by web search.

## Sources
- https://stonewhistle.com/ and instrument pages /instruments/innato/, natey/, zenflute/, double/, ova-fluit/, /faqs/
- Prices: shop feed https://stonewhistle.myshopify.com/products.json (shop currency EUR, via /meta.json); product pages checked HTTP 200.
- Double Flute price: reseller https://www.onetone.one/products/double-flute (555).
- Stonewhistle's own pages show no prices (stonewhistle.com buy buttons go to the shop); the figures come from the shop feed.

## Caveats
- The maker's range is only five families, so 7 items are model/size variants.
- No breath-free instruments exist in this catalog.
- Fragile clay, not washable (soft dry cloth only, no chemicals); guarantee 6 months for defects; wait 3-5 months.
- Site says nothing about disability suitability: ask info@stonewhistle.com. Hand sizes S/M/L matter.
- Innato Mini: site page says D5, shop title says C5; shop title used.
- The shop product images are shared per family (one picture for all Natey sizes, etc.).

## Rejected
- OvA Flute C2: 1500 EUR, 7 kg, 27x44 cm, hard to handle and costly.
- Other Natey/Innato keys: lower keys cost more and are bigger; A4/E4 chosen for price and size.
- Other candidates seen in search, not chosen: Thomann Shaman Flute (wood, mixed reviews), Schlagwerk (chimes, no flutes), Sensatonics, Kirstein, Lark in the Morning stone flute (fragile).
